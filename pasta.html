Creamy Garlic Pasta

★★★★★ 5.0

Ingredients

  • 12 oz pasta
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • ½ cup Parmesan cheese
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • ¼ teaspoon black pepper
  • Fresh parsley

Instructions

  1. Cook the pasta in salted boiling water until al dente.
  2. Heat olive oil in a skillet over medium heat.
  3. Add garlic and cook until fragrant.
  4. Add cream and simmer gently.
  5. Stir in Parmesan, salt, and pepper.
  6. Add the cooked pasta and toss until coated.
  7. Finish with fresh parsley and serve.

Recipe Tip

Save a little pasta water. A splash can help loosen the sauce if it becomes too thick.
