RECIPE CARD

Creamy Garlic Pasta

A creamy and comforting pasta dish that's ready in about 25 minutes.

★★★★★ 5.0 Easy family favorite

Ingredients

  • 12 oz pasta
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • ½ cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • ¼ teaspoon black pepper
  • Fresh parsley, for serving

Instructions

  1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Cook the pasta according to the package directions until al dente.
  2. While the pasta cooks, heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat.
  3. Add the minced garlic and cook gently until fragrant.
  4. Pour in the cream and stir gently. Let the sauce simmer for a few minutes.
  5. Add the Parmesan cheese, salt, and black pepper. Stir until the sauce becomes smooth and creamy.
  6. Drain the pasta and add it to the skillet. Toss everything together until the pasta is completely coated.
  7. Finish with fresh parsley and serve immediately.

Recipe Tips

Save a little pasta water before draining. If the sauce becomes too thick, add a small splash of pasta water and stir until it reaches the consistency you like.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use another type of pasta?

Yes. Spaghetti, fettuccine, penne, or your favorite pasta shape can work well.

Can I make this recipe ahead?

This pasta is best served fresh, but leftovers can be stored in the refrigerator and gently reheated.

What can I serve with it?

A simple green salad or roasted vegetables make an easy side dish.
