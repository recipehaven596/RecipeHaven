Garlic Herb Roasted Pumpkin

Tender roasted pumpkin with crispy caramelized edges, garlic, rosemary, and thyme. An easy healthy fall side dish ready in about 40 minutes.

★★★★★ 5.0 Easy fall favorite

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs (900 g) pumpkin, peeled, seeded, and cut into 1-inch cubes
  • 2 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil
  • 3 garlic cloves, finely minced
  • 1 teaspoon dried rosemary
  • 1 teaspoon dried thyme
  • ½ teaspoon paprika
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • ¼ teaspoon freshly ground black pepper
  • 1 tablespoon fresh parsley, chopped
  • 1 tablespoon pumpkin seeds, optional
  • 1 teaspoon grated Parmesan, optional

Instructions

  1. Step 1: Preheat the oven to 400°F (200°C). Line a large baking sheet with parchment paper.
  2. Step 2: Peel and remove the seeds from the pumpkin, then cut it into evenly sized cubes.
  3. Step 3: Place the pumpkin in a large bowl. Add olive oil, minced garlic, rosemary, thyme, paprika, salt, and black pepper. Toss until every piece is evenly coated.
  4. Step 4: Spread the pumpkin cubes on the baking sheet in a single layer. Avoid overcrowding so the pumpkin can roast instead of steam.
  5. Step 5: Bake for 30–35 minutes, turning the pieces halfway through, until tender inside and golden brown with caramelized edges.
  6. Step 6: Remove from the oven and sprinkle with fresh parsley and pumpkin seeds. Add a little Parmesan if desired.
  7. Step 7: Serve immediately while warm as a side dish with chicken, fish, rice, or a fresh salad.

Recipe Tips

For the best caramelized edges, spread the pumpkin pieces in a single layer and avoid overcrowding the baking sheet. Turn the pieces halfway through roasting for even browning.

Frequently Asked Questions

What temperature should I roast the pumpkin?

Roast the pumpkin at 400°F (200°C) for about 30–35 minutes, turning the pieces halfway through cooking.

How do I get crispy caramelized edges?

Spread the pumpkin in a single layer and avoid overcrowding the baking sheet. This allows the pumpkin to roast instead of steam.

Can I add Parmesan?

Yes. A small amount of grated Parmesan can be added after roasting for extra savory flavor.

What can I serve with roasted pumpkin?

It works well with roasted chicken, grilled fish, rice, or a fresh salad.
