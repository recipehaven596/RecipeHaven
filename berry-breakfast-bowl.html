Berry Breakfast Bowl

Ingredients

  • 2 cups Greek yogurt
  • 1 cup mixed berries
  • 1 banana, sliced
  • ¼ cup granola
  • 1 tablespoon honey
  • 1 tablespoon chia seeds

Instructions

  1. Divide the Greek yogurt between two bowls.
  2. Add the mixed berries over the yogurt.
  3. Add the sliced banana.
  4. Sprinkle granola and chia seeds over the top.
  5. Drizzle with honey.
  6. Serve immediately and enjoy.

Recipe Tip

For the best texture, add the granola just before serving so it stays crunchy.

GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use frozen berries?

Yes. Frozen berries can work well. Let them thaw slightly before adding them to the bowl.

Can I prepare it ahead of time?

You can prepare the yogurt and fruit ahead of time, but add the granola just before serving.

Can I change the toppings?

Absolutely. You can use your favorite fruit, seeds, nuts, or granola.
