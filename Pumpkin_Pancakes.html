Pumpkin Pancakes

Fluffy pumpkin pancakes with warm fall spices, ready in just 25 minutes.

★★★★★ 5.0 Cozy family breakfast

Ingredients

  • 1½ cups (190 g) all-purpose flour
  • 2 teaspoons baking powder
  • ½ teaspoon baking soda
  • ¼ teaspoon salt
  • 1½ teaspoons ground cinnamon
  • ½ teaspoon ground nutmeg
  • ¼ teaspoon ground ginger
  • 1 tablespoon brown sugar
  • 1 large egg
  • 1 cup (240 ml) whole milk
  • ½ cup (120 g) pumpkin purée
  • 2 tablespoons melted unsalted butter
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

For Serving

  • Maple syrup
  • Butter
  • Chopped pecans
  • A sprinkle of cinnamon

Instructions

  1. Step 1: Mix the dry ingredients: In a large bowl, whisk together the flour, baking powder, baking soda, salt, cinnamon, nutmeg, ginger, and brown sugar.
  2. Step 2: Mix the wet ingredients: In a separate bowl, whisk together the egg, milk, pumpkin purée, melted butter, and vanilla extract until smooth.
  3. Step 3: Combine: Pour the wet mixture into the dry ingredients. Stir gently until just combined. A few small lumps are perfectly fine—avoid overmixing.
  4. Step 4: Heat the pan: Place a non-stick skillet or griddle over medium heat.

Serving Ideas

Serve the pancakes warm with maple syrup, a little butter, chopped pecans, and a sprinkle of cinnamon for a cozy fall breakfast.
