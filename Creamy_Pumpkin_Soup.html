Creamy Pumpkin Soup

A smooth and comforting pumpkin soup made with simple ingredients and ready in 35 minutes.

★★★★★ 5.0 Easy family favorite

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs (900 g) pumpkin, peeled and cubed
  • 1 medium yellow onion, chopped
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 4 cups (950 ml) vegetable broth
  • ½ cup (120 ml) coconut milk or heavy cream
  • ½ tsp ground nutmeg
  • ½ tsp ground cinnamon (optional)
  • Salt, to taste
  • Black pepper, to taste
  • Fresh thyme or parsley, for garnish
  • Pumpkin seeds (pepitas), for garnish

Instructions

  1. Step 1: Heat the olive oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add the chopped onion and cook for about 5 minutes until soft and translucent.
  2. Step 2: Add the minced garlic and cook for 1 minute, stirring constantly.
  3. Step 3: Add the pumpkin cubes, nutmeg, cinnamon (if using), salt, and black pepper. Stir everything together for 2–3 minutes.
  4. Step 4: Pour in the vegetable broth and bring to a boil.
  5. Step 5: Reduce the heat, cover, and simmer for 20 minutes, or until the pumpkin is fork-tender.
  6. Step 6: Blend the soup until completely smooth using an immersion blender or a regular blender.
  7. Step 7: Stir in the coconut milk or heavy cream and heat gently for 2 minutes without boiling.
  8. Step 8: Serve warm and garnish with pumpkin seeds, fresh thyme, and a drizzle of coconut milk or cream.

Recipe Tips

For a silky smooth soup, blend thoroughly before adding the coconut milk or heavy cream. Adjust the thickness with a little extra vegetable broth if needed.

GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use canned pumpkin?

Yes. Canned pumpkin can be used when fresh pumpkin is not available. Choose plain pumpkin puree rather than sweetened pumpkin pie filling.

Can I use coconut milk instead of heavy cream?

Yes. Coconut milk gives the soup a creamy texture while keeping the flavor light and slightly nutty.

How do I make the soup thinner?

Add a little extra vegetable broth and stir until the soup reaches your preferred consistency.
