Cozy Vegetable Soup

A warm and comforting vegetable soup packed with colorful vegetables and simple homemade flavors.

Prep: 15 min Cook: 30 min Total: 45 min Servings: 4

Ingredients

  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 medium onion, diced
  • 2 carrots, sliced
  • 2 celery stalks, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 4 cups vegetable broth
  • 2 cups diced tomatoes
  • 1 cup green beans
  • 1 teaspoon dried Italian herbs
  • Salt to taste
  • Black pepper to taste
  • Fresh parsley for serving

How to Make Cozy Vegetable Soup

1. Sauté the vegetables

Heat the olive oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add the onion, carrots, and celery. Cook until the vegetables begin to soften.

2. Add the garlic

Add the minced garlic and cook for about one minute, until fragrant.

3. Add the broth and tomatoes

Pour in the vegetable broth and diced tomatoes. Stir everything together.

4. Add the remaining vegetables

Add the green beans and dried Italian herbs.

5. Simmer

Bring the soup to a gentle boil. Reduce the heat and simmer until the vegetables are tender.

6. Season and serve

Season with salt and black pepper. Serve warm and garnish with fresh parsley.

RECIPE NOTES

A cozy bowl for any day.

This vegetable soup is simple, comforting, and easy to make. It is a great option for a relaxed family dinner or a cozy evening at home.

SERVING IDEA

Make it a complete meal.

Serve the soup with warm bread, a simple salad, or your favorite side dish for a satisfying dinner.
