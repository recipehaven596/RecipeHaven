RECIPE CARD

Maple Roasted Squash

Ingredients

  • 1 medium winter squash
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 2 tablespoons maple syrup
  • ½ teaspoon cinnamon
  • ¼ teaspoon salt
  • ¼ teaspoon black pepper

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 400°F.
  2. Peel and cut the squash into even pieces.
  3. Place the squash on a lined baking sheet.
  4. Toss with olive oil, maple syrup, cinnamon, salt, and pepper.
  5. Roast until tender and golden around the edges.
  6. Serve warm.

Recipe Tip

Cut the squash into similar-sized pieces so everything cooks evenly.

GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use another squash?

Yes. Many winter squash varieties can work with this method.

Can I prepare it ahead?

You can cut and season the squash ahead of time and roast it when ready.
