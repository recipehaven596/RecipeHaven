Lemon Herb Chicken

Ingredients

  • 4 boneless chicken breasts
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1 teaspoon dried thyme
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Fresh parsley for serving

Instructions

  1. Season the chicken with salt, pepper, oregano, and thyme.
  2. Heat olive oil in a large skillet.
  3. Add the chicken and cook until golden and cooked through.
  4. Add the minced garlic and cook briefly until fragrant.
  5. Squeeze fresh lemon juice over the chicken.
  6. Garnish with fresh parsley and serve.

Recipe Tip

Let the chicken rest for a few minutes before serving to help keep it juicy.
