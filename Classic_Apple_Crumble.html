Classic Apple Crumble

A warm and comforting apple crumble made with tender cinnamon apples and a golden, buttery oat topping. An easy homemade dessert perfect for cozy evenings and family gatherings.

PREP TIME 15 minutes
COOK TIME 35 minutes
TOTAL TIME 50 minutes
SERVINGS 6
CATEGORY Dessert

This classic apple crumble is one of those simple desserts that makes the whole kitchen smell amazing. Sweet apples are tossed with cinnamon and baked underneath a buttery golden oat topping.

Serve it warm with vanilla ice cream, whipped cream, or simply enjoy it on its own for a cozy homemade dessert.

Classic Apple Crumble

Tender cinnamon apples baked under a golden, buttery oat crumble topping.

★★★★★ 5.0 Easy family favorite

Ingredients

For the Apple Filling

  • 6 medium apples, peeled and sliced
  • 2 tablespoons brown sugar
  • 1 tablespoon all-purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
  • ½ teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice

For the Crumble Topping

  • ¾ cup (70 g) rolled oats
  • ½ cup (65 g) all-purpose flour
  • ½ cup (100 g) brown sugar
  • ½ teaspoon ground cinnamon
  • ¼ teaspoon salt
  • 6 tablespoons (85 g) unsalted butter, melted
  • ½ cup chopped pecans, optional

Instructions

  1. Step 1: Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C). Lightly grease a baking dish.
  2. Step 2: Peel, core, and slice the apples. Place them in a large bowl.
  3. Step 3: Add brown sugar, flour, cinnamon, vanilla extract, and lemon juice. Toss until the apples are evenly coated.
  4. Step 4: Transfer the apple mixture to the prepared baking dish and spread evenly.
  5. Step 5: In a separate bowl, combine oats, flour, brown sugar, cinnamon, and salt.
  6. Step 6: Add the melted butter and mix until the mixture becomes crumbly.
  7. Step 7: Sprinkle the crumble topping evenly over the apples.
  8. Step 8: Bake for 30–35 minutes, until the apples are tender and the topping is golden brown.
  9. Step 9: Allow the crumble to cool for about 5–10 minutes before serving.
  10. Step 10: Serve warm with vanilla ice cream, whipped cream, or a sprinkle of cinnamon.

Recipe Tips

For the best texture, choose apples that hold their shape when baked. A combination of sweet and slightly tart apples gives the crumble a delicious balance of flavors.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use different apples?

Yes. You can use Granny Smith, Honeycrisp, Gala, or a combination of your favorite baking apples.

Can I make apple crumble ahead of time?

Yes. Prepare the crumble ahead of time and refrigerate it before baking. Add a few extra minutes to the baking time if needed.

What should I serve with apple crumble?

Warm apple crumble is delicious with vanilla ice cream, whipped cream, or a drizzle of caramel sauce.
