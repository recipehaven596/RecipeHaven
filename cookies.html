Chocolate Chip Cookies

Ingredients

  • ½ cup softened butter
  • ½ cup brown sugar
  • ¼ cup granulated sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1½ cups all-purpose flour
  • ½ teaspoon baking soda
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • 1 cup chocolate chips

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 350°F.
  2. Cream the butter and sugars until smooth.
  3. Mix in the egg and vanilla.
  4. Add flour, baking soda, and salt.
  5. Fold in the chocolate chips.
  6. Scoop the dough onto a lined baking sheet.
  7. Bake until the edges are lightly golden.
  8. Let the cookies cool before serving.

Recipe Tip

Letting the cookies cool on the baking sheet for a few minutes helps them set while keeping the center tender.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I freeze the cookie dough?

Yes. Portion the dough and freeze it in an airtight container.

Why are my cookies spreading?

Butter that is too warm can make cookie dough spread more during baking.
